# MedWeave AI: Orchestrating the Future of Intelligent Healthcare

**Hackathon Category**: AI in Healthcare / Operational Excellence / HealthTech
**Tagline**: Bridging the Clinical-Administrative Divide with Deterministic AI Agents.

---

## 🏆 Vision & Mission
MedWeave AI was built to solve the two biggest crises in modern medicine: **Physician Burnout** (caused by administrative "paperwork") and **Patient Safety** (risks during high-pressure consultations). By weaving together real-time clinical intelligence and automated healthcare operations, we enable doctors to focus on what matters most—the patient.

## 🔴 The Problem
1.  **Clinical Risk**: In high-pressure environments, 10-15% of clinical errors occur due to missed symptoms or inappropriate prescriptions during live sessions.
2.  **Administrative Friction**: Healthcare providers spend over **15.6 hours per week** on administrative tasks.
3.  **Revenue Cycle Inefficiency**: Claims adjudication and prior authorizations are often "black boxes," leading to a 5-10% denial rate due to simple coding errors.

## 🚀 The Solution: MedWeave AI
MedWeave AI is a unified platform that deploys specialized, deterministic AI agents to handle the heavy lifting of healthcare.

### 1. The Clinical Guardian (Live Agent)
- **Real-time Transcription & Auditing**: Monitors live sessions to detect red flags or misdiagnoses instantly.
- **Missed Question Detector**: An active "clinical co-pilot" that suggests follow-up questions specialized to the patient’s symptoms.
- **Prescription Safety Vault**: Automatically cross-references new prescriptions against the patient’s clinical history and allergies.

### 2. The Operations Hub (Workflow Agents)
- **Medical Coding Agent**: Transforms messy clinical notes into standardized **ICD-10** and **CPT** codes with an auditable reasoning trail.
- **Deterministic Adjudication Engine**: A world-first 6-step AI workflow that validates claims against payer policies (e.g., BlueCross v4.1) and calculates the **Insurance vs. Patient** financial split with 100% logic transparency.
- **Prior Auth Automated Agent**: Drastically reduces wait times for high-cost procedures by automating clinical justification checks.

## 🛠️ Technical Innovation & Execution
Our project stands out due to its **deterministic approach** to AI. Unlike standard chatbots, MedWeave AI uses:
1.  **Deterministic Logic (Temp 0)**: All operational and financial agents operate at Temperature 0 to ensure mathematical consistency and reliability.
2.  **Schema-Driven Reasoning**: We use strict **Pydantic schemas** to enforce structured AI outputs, making the "AI black box" fully auditable for human-in-the-loop verification.
3.  **Frontend/Backend Synergy**: A high-performance **FastAPI** backend coupled with a premium **Next.js** frontend designed with modern "Medical Aesthetics" (Glassmorphism, Micro-animations).

### **The Full Tech Stack**

#### **Core AI & Intelligence**
- **LLM**: Google Gemini Pro (State-of-the-art Generative AI).
- **Agent Framework**: Custom Deterministic Architectures (Temperature 0).
- **Prompt Engineering**: Schema-driven system instructions.

#### **Backend (Python Ecosystem)**
- **Framework**: **FastAPI** (High-performance, asynchronous web framework).
- **Server**: **Uvicorn** (ASGI server for lightning-fast request handling).
- **ORM**: **SQLAlchemy 2.0** (Advanced Database Object Relational Mapper).
- **Validation**: **Pydantic v2** (Strict data parsing and validation).
- **Security**: **JWT (JSON Web Tokens)** via Python-JOSE for secure doctor authentication.
- **Database**: **SQLite** (Configured for immediate scalability and edge deployment).

#### **Frontend (Modern Web Stack)**
- **Framework**: **Next.js 16** (App Router, Server Components).
- **UI Library**: **React 19** (Concurrent Rendering).
- **Icons**: **Lucide React** (Premium clinical iconography).
- **Animations**: **Framer Motion** (Smooth transitions & micro-animations).
- **Styling**: **Vanilla CSS3** (Custom Medical Design System) + **Tailwind CSS 4** utilities.
- **API Client**: **Axios** (Robust HTTP communication).

#### **DevOps & Tooling**
- **Environment**: Python 3.12 / Node.js 20.
- **Version Control**: Git.
- **Management**: NPM / Pip.
