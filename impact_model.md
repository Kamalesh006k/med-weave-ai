## 2. Quantified Impact Model (Assumptions)

**Assumptions**:
- **Clinic Size**: 10 Doctors, 200 Patients/Day.
- **Provider Hourly Rate**: Rs. 1,000 / Hr.
- **Admin Hourly Rate**: Rs. 300 / Hr.
- **Avg. Claim Value**: Rs. 5,000.

### **2.1. Direct Cost Reduction (Administrative)**
- **Manual Tasks**: Transcription, coding, and adjudication.
- **Time Saved**: 12 minutes/patient × 200 patients = 40 hours/day.
- **Daily Savings**: 40 hours × Rs. 300 = **Rs. 12,000 / Day**.
- **Annual Savings (300 days)**: **Rs. 3.6 Million**.

### **2.2. Revenue Recovery (Denied Claims)**
- **Claim Denials**: 12% of 200 daily claims (24 claims) denied due to coding errors.
- **Recovered Revenue**: Reducing denials to 2% saves 20 claims/day.
- **Daily Recovery**: 20 claims × Rs. 5,000 = **Rs. 100,000 / Day**.
- **Annual Recovery**: **Rs. 30 Million**.

## 3. The "MedWeave AI" Advantage: Qualitative Impact

### **A. Clinical Safety (The Guardian Agent)**
By providing real-time risk detection during live sessions, MedWeave AI reduces the **"Malpractice Liability Risk"**—a cost that is difficult to quantify but critical for physician peace of mind.

### **B. Patient Experience**
- **Transparency**: Instant adjudication means patients know their out-of-pocket costs (Rs.) before they leave the clinic.
- **Speed**: Prior Authorizations are processed in minutes, not days, meaning faster treatment for critical cases.

### **C. Auditability**
The 6-step deterministic logic provides a permanent, auditable reasoning trail. This simplifies compliance with healthcare regulations and reduces the cost of audits.
